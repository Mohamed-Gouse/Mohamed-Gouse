## About Me

I'm **Mohamed Gouse**, a Software Engineer focused on building practical, scalable software — from backend platforms and APIs to complete full-stack applications.

My core engineering work centres on **Python, Django, Django REST Framework, JavaScript and React**. I also work with Next.js, Node.js, databases, background processing, cloud deployment and third-party integrations.

A significant part of my professional experience is in **PropTech and Real Estate Technology**, including property inventory, CRM, portal integrations, lead workflows, property management and internal business systems.

- Building backend services, APIs and full-stack products.
- Turning operational workflows into practical software.
- Integrating external platforms, CRMs and communication services.
- Developing systems for property, inventory, HR and business operations.
- Continuously improving application architecture, usability and delivery.
